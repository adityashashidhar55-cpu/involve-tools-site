Every tool here works inside your browser. What you type stays on this page. Nothing is sent to us unless you press one of four buttons, and each one tells you first what it sends: the email forms (they send what you typed), the essay “deep read” (it sends your essay), sending one interview recording for review (it sends that one recording), and signing in to the interview room (it sends your email address). These go to our own server in Mumbai. For the deep read, that server may pass your essay to an AI model to read it. Nothing else leaves this page. This site loads nothing from other companies: no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no outside fonts. If a school does not publish a number, we leave it blank. We never guess.
