Every tool here runs in your browser. Your essay, your salary figures and your recordings are read and processed in this tab, and they stay in it unless you press one of three buttons that tell you, before you press them, exactly what they send: the email forms, which send what you typed into them; the deep read on the essay page, which sends the draft you wrote; and sending one interview take to a consultant for review, which sends that single recording and no other. Each of those lists what it sends first, sends nothing until you ask, and says so in plain words when the service behind it is not switched on. All three are connected today. What you type into an email form reaches our own server in Mumbai, which keeps your name, your address, the fields that form listed and the exact sentence you ticked, and which tells you on screen if it cannot yet send the email you asked for. The deep read sends the draft you wrote to that same server, which passes it to a language model only when one is configured and tells you on screen, in those words, when one is not. Sending a take for review uploads that one recording, and no other, to private storage, and gives you a reference you can use to have it deleted. None of them does anything until you press its own button. Nothing else leaves this tab. The one thing this page fetches is Plausible Analytics: a cookieless counter that records page views and five events — the home page being opened, a tool being opened, a tool producing a result, a click on a link to us and an email form being submitted. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, follows you nowhere else, and holds no identifier for you, so we can see that a tool was finished and not who finished it. Never a figure, a word or a file you entered. No fonts, no trackers, no advertising pixels, no images loaded from elsewhere: the typefaces and the mark are inside this page, and the build refuses to ship if anything else appears. Where a figure has no published source, we leave it blank rather than estimate it.
